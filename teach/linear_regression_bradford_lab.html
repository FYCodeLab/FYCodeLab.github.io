What is happening mathematically?

A Bradford calibration relates known protein concentration to measured optical density. Your job is to estimate the straight line that best represents the trend.

What is a residual?

A residual is the vertical difference between a measured point and the OD predicted by your line at the same concentration.

How should you place the line?

  • Try to pass through the centre of the cloud.
  • Avoid putting nearly all points on the same side.
  • Large vertical errors matter more than small ones.
  • Do not simply connect the first and last data points.

What does least squares do?

It chooses the slope and intercept that minimize Σ(y − ŷ)². Squaring makes large residuals count strongly.

Bradford assay context

The calibration line converts optical density into an estimate of protein concentration. A poor calibration line therefore produces poor concentration estimates.
