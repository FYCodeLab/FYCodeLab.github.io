What is happening inside?

Your camera image first passes through MobileNet, a convolutional neural network already trained to recognize useful visual patterns. We reuse its internal features instead of training a large vision network from scratch.

1. Pixels The webcam supplies an image made of RGB pixel values.
2. MobileNet Convolutional layers transform the image into a compact feature vector.
3. Neurons A small trainable neural network learns how your two classes differ.
4. Softmax The final layer converts scores into probabilities that sum to 100%.
